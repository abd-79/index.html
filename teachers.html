Department 01

Department of Natural & Physical Sciences

The Natural Sciences faculty is composed of senior professors, assistant professors, and certified laboratory demonstrators who specialize in teaching Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. The team bridges theoretical concepts with rigorous laboratory experiments to prepare students for FBISE board examinations and competitive medical and engineering university entrance tests (MDCAT/ECAT).

Physics Wing

Mechanics, electromagnetism, modern physics, and FBISE practical apparatus training.

Chemistry Wing

Organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry supported by dedicated chemical testing labs.

Biology Wing

Botany, zoology, cytology, and dissection practices under qualified life-science demonstrators.

Department 02

Department of Computer Science & IT

In step with rapid technological evolution, our Computer Science educators equip intermediate (ICS) and secondary school students with foundational programming knowledge, algorithmic thinking, and computer architecture principles.

Hands-on Coding
Computer Lab Instruction

Practical sessions conducted in modern computer laboratories with high-speed internet and networked terminals under regular faculty supervision.

C/C++, Database Systems & IT Concepts
Curriculum Mastery
Modern IT Education

Targeted FBISE syllabus preparation ensuring students build theoretical clarity alongside functional software understanding.

Board Exam Practice & Mock Coding Tests
Department 03

Department of Mathematics

Mathematics is the universal language of the sciences. Our mathematics lecturers focus on eliminating students' hesitation with numbers by breaking down complex concepts into intuitive, manageable steps.

Conceptual Clarity
Algebra, Calculus & Geometry

Step-by-step guidance across intermediate mathematics, covering functions, limits, integration, differentiation, and coordinate geometry.

Comprehensive FBISE Textbook Coverage
Practice Clinics
Daily Problem Solving

Regular weekly quizzes, past-paper drills, and formula reviews that build speed, accuracy, and confidence for annual board exams.

Past Paper Analysis & Formula Sheets
Department 04

Department of Languages & Humanities

Our Humanities and Language faculty instills linguistic eloquence, critical expression, and a strong understanding of national culture, ethics, and history across English, Urdu, Islamic Studies, and Pakistan Studies.

Bilingual Fluency
English & Urdu Language

Developing essay writing, comprehension, grammar mastery, and speechcraft. Faculty also mentors students for college debates and literary publications.

Essay Composition, Poetry & Declamation
Civic & Moral Values
Islamiat & Pakistan Studies

Grounding students in ethical character, national heritage, geographical understanding, and constitutional awareness.

Historical Heritage & Moral Character
Support System

Academic Counseling & Mentorship

Education at IMCB G-10/4 is not confined to standard lecture hours. Our faculty maintains active counseling channels to ensure students receive the personal attention they need to flourish.

Remedial Classes

Special after-school coaching sessions for board examination candidates needing extra concept reinforcement.

Parent-Teacher Links

Regular PTMs allowing teachers and guardians to review attendance, disciplinary standards, and academic performance.

Proctorial Guidance

Faculty proctors work closely with the Prefectorial Board to preserve campus discipline, punctuality, and mutual respect.
